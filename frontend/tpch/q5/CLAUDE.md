## TPC-H Definition (§2.4.5 — "Local Supplier Volume")

```sql
SELECT  n_name,
        SUM(l_extendedprice * (1 - l_discount)) AS revenue
FROM    customer, orders, lineitem, supplier, nation, region
WHERE   c_custkey   = o_custkey
  AND   l_orderkey  = o_orderkey
  AND   l_suppkey   = s_suppkey
  AND   c_nationkey = s_nationkey
  AND   s_nationkey = n_nationkey
  AND   n_regionkey = r_regionkey
  AND   r_name      = ':1'
  AND   o_orderdate >= ':d'
  AND   o_orderdate <  ':d' + INTERVAL '1' YEAR
GROUP BY n_name
ORDER BY revenue DESC;
```

### Substitution Parameters

| Parameter | Domain | Description |
|-----------|--------|-------------|
| `:1` | One of REGION.r_name (AFRICA, AMERICA, ASIA, EUROPE, MIDDLE EAST) | Region filter |
| `:d` | January 1 of a year in [1993, 1997] | Start of the 1-year order window |

**Validation values**: REGION = ASIA, DATE = 1994-01-01.

### Real-world meaning

Q5 measures, for each nation in a chosen region, the revenue
generated by lineitems where the customer and the supplier are both
located in that nation — i.e. *local* commerce. The constraint
`c_nationkey = s_nationkey` is the linchpin: it filters out
cross-border trade and keeps only intra-nation sales, so the result
reads as "how much business does each nation in this region do with
itself?". The business question is whether to open a local
distribution centre in a given country, where high local-revenue
nations are the strongest candidates.

## Plan & execution sketch

Q5 is a **6-table join** with three small dimension tables and the
familiar customer × orders × lineitem trunk plus SUPPLIER joining
LINEITEM on `l_suppkey` and gating on the cross-equality
`c_nationkey = s_nationkey`.

- **REGION** (5 rows) and **NATION** (25 rows) are loaded into
  in-memory hashmaps up-front. REGION is filtered by `r_name = :1`
  to a single `r_regionkey`; NATION is then narrowed to nations in
  that region (~5 rows) and indexed by `n_nationkey → n_name`.
- **SUPPLIER** is built as an in-memory hashmap keyed on
  `s_suppkey`, restricted to suppliers whose `s_nationkey` is in
  the in-region NATION set (so non-region suppliers are pruned at
  build time).
- **OL pipeline** drives the main scan. For each lineitem:
  1. Look up SUPPLIER by `l_suppkey`; skip if absent (non-region).
  2. Look up CUSTOMER by `o_custkey` (via the orders side of the
     join); skip if absent or if
     `c_nationkey != s_nationkey`.
  3. Resolve `n_name` via the NATION map and accumulate
     `l_extendedprice * (1 - l_discount)` into the per-nation
     revenue bucket.
- Apply orderdate window during the orders side of the join.
- Group-by is on `n_name` (~5 buckets after the region filter); no
  LIMIT.

## Storage-structure variant axis

Same S1–S4 convention as Q3 / Q12 — see the top-level
[`../CLAUDE.md §Storage-structure → Wrapper Mapping`](../CLAUDE.md#storage-structure--wrapper-mapping).
Q5 has **no** invoice extension, so S5 (aCOLI) is not applicable;
the COL MI used by Q3 is also unlikely to help Q5 because the join
graph is not pure-hierarchical (SUPPLIER attaches to LINEITEM by
suppkey, not by custkey/orderkey). The natural MI for Q5 is the
existing `MI(ORDERS, LINEITEM)` from `OrdersLineitemPipeline` plus
SUPPLIER as a hash side; this matches the Q3/Q12/Q9 footprint.
